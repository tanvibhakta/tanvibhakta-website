Review of [Sable Cafe](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Prc2BfkLkktLejPe6) in indiranagar
1. mediocre Chimichurri Lamb Burger (the chimichurri 404 and lamb patty was too gamey). fries were single fried, sauce was probably from a packet (and cold)
2. surprisingly decent americano - beans felt oily in a good way.
3. music generic and loud
4. Strong AC, and outside next to a place that kept smoking something.

overall: skip
