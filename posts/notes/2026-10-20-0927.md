Let me explain the "hooks" thing.

Most learning happens in our brain by "mapping" what is new to something that we already know. We can take advantage of this by intentionally crafting metaphors that map the new thing to something we already have an intuition about. For example, flowing water is a pretty decent concept to map the new concept of electricity to. But if you have a background in mechanical physics, potential energy is a better one, because there are more similarities.
