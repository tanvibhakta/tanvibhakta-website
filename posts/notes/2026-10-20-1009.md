Next the course tackles procrastination - something I think everyone who wants to do anything struggles with, especially in this era of dopamine deficits.

Procrastination becomes such a problem because its a habit. If we understand how habits are formed, we can learn to build better ones, thereby increasing our ability to learn (because remember, the most efficient way to learn is to do something consistently).
