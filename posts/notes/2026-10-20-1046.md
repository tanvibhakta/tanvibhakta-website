After you've identified your cue and done your thing, figure out a way to (sustainably!) give your brain a thing that will give it dopamine. I have [my rewards framework](https://tanvibhakta.in/blog/minting-tanvi-bucks/), of course - but for most everyday things I just take a minute to bask in the task well done. I know folks who love checking off a box - that act of using the pencil or the animation of the task disappearing from your list can become its own reward.
