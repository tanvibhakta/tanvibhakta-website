Habits are so important because humans have limited reserves of willpower in a day. We don't want to spend it all on (willing ourselves to) doing things. By doing the important stuff (hygiene, work, learning, food) on routine (habit) we're saving the most precious resources for what matters.

By breaking down a habit into its component parts, you only have to put effort into applying willpower to attach the routine to the cue. Also that old adage of "build a habit in 21 days!" is not true. It takes closer to 90 days for any habit to be fully ingrained into your life.
