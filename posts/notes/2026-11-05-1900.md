I have come across many small quirky internet publications, wanted to keep up with them, and then forgotten all about them. Luckily, when I found Goya, I had just set up my RSS feed manager.

Goya mostly does small, quick features about the niche dishes from all kinds of India. I learn so much about the culture in this country through the narratives Goya chooses to feature with food as a proxy. Every so often, they will do a longer, small-ideas-big-change feature like this one: https://www.goya.in/blog/not-an-inherited-feast-the-modest-archives-of-my-mothers-kitchen

I'm glad for internet publications, glad for food plurality, glad for the power of storytelling, and glad for the existence of my feed reader to bring it all to me and brighten my day.
