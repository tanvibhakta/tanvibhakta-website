other things that help in the pursuit of habit building

1\. pomodoros
2\. eating your frogs early in the day (do the hard thing first, when your willpower reserves are still fresh)
3\. making your todo list for tomorrow before you leave for today anchors tomorrow in your mind and keeps momentum going
4\. [just do the thing](https://tanvibhakta.in/blog/what-my-hands-learn-every-time-i-log-in/#p-6), even if it feels hard - its not going to be.
5\. focus on the process of building the habit, not the outcome (I'm going to do 10 squats, not I'm going to have sore knees and thighs)
