description: "disclosure on how I use llms (tldr: not for writing)"
publishedOn: 2026-10-20
---

I am an AI engineer - that is, I work at an organisation where we build products that take advantage of LLM capabilities. At work, I exclusively write code via LLMs.

In my regular life, I use llms to automate a lot of the boring stuff. I also use llms as a force multiplier for efforts I am a part of. I’m a fan of using llms to practice digital decluttering.

Writing is fun, and thinking is very fun. Therefore, **I will never use llms to write on this blog**. I may ask an llm for feedback and editorial opinions, but I will never copy-paste from an llm into my post.

I think widespread llm usage is here to stay. There’s a lot of anger against authoritative systems, and against big tech. I see llms as a tool that makes it easier to craft a world that works for each individual.

I am here for the personal software revolution that will show up when tokens are cheap to generate. I don’t doubt that we’ll get there soon. In the process, I want to stay true to what brings me joy as a human: Building things. Connecting with people. Solving problems. Having fun.

## tldr: not for writing, yes for most other things.
