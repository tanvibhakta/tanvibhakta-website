Like most people, I have a complicated relationship with my birthday. This year, it didn't manifest. 

## What I did

My birthday fell on a Sunday! And the Saturday was a gfgf - so I took myself out to a good party, dressing up, looking hot, and knowing that I can show myself a good time. I went alone (didn't have the energy to make plans with friends) but immediately met 12 people i know (bangalore queer circles are MUCH bigger than they were in 2016, but they're still small enough! What can I say) and really just danced. Bodies feel so good when they do what you want. 

I woke up on Sunday with a mild hangover and a craving for Ramen, so I took myself to Kuuraku, did some shopping after, and came back home just in time for my friends to slowly show up to a small birthday hang shru had very kindly decided to manage for me. I hung out with my closest friends, then changed into my pajamas and went to bed at 10pm. Fantastic day, couldn't have asked for more.
