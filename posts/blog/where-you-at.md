I really like this set of questions from [z1nz0l1n](https://z1nz0l1n.com/26w44/) that makes up this month's IndieWeb Carnival topic!

> What follows is a self-scoring test on basic environmental perception of place

I'm modifying it to be Bangalore-specific, and proposing it as a [prompt](https://blr.indiewebclub.org/prompts/) for [IndieWebClub Blr #38](https://blr.indiewebclub.org/events/indiewebclub-38/).

1. Trace the water you drink from precipitation to tap.
2. How many days till the moon is full? (Slack of two days allowed.)
3. What was on the soil where your home is built? What kind of soil is it built on?
4. What was the total rainfall in your area last year (calendar year)? (Slack: 1″ for every 20″.)
5. What is the coolest area in a 4km radius of your house during the peak of summer?
6. Where is the air quality monitor for your neighborhood situated?
7. Name five edible plants native to Bangalore and their season(s) of availability.
8. From what direction do the monsoon rains generally come in Bangalore? What about post-monsoon rains? What about off-season rains?
9. Where does your garbage go? How does it get there?
10. What is the growing season for carbohydrate you typically eat?
11. On what day of the year are the shadows the shortest in Bangalore?
12. What are the breeding seasons for animals you depend on? (Typically, cows, bulls, chickens, goats, cats and dogs)
13. Name five grasses in your area. Are any of them native?
14. Name five resident and five migratory birds in your area.
15. What is the land use history of where you live?
16. What primary ecological event/process influenced the land form where you live? (Bonus special: what’s the evidence?)
17. What species have become extinct in your area?
18. What are the major plant associations in your region? Animal associations? Bird associations?
19. From where you’re reading this, point north.
20. What is the season of bloom of your favorite Bangalore flower?
