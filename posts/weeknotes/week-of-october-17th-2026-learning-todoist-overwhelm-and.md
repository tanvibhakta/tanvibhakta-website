## Life

My to do list (literally, the app) had somehow become a mix of things I must do to survive + dumb things I don't want to forget. The rest of my everyday tasks stayed in my head. This week, i finally put all of those tasks down in the app too! Instant catatonia. I got so overwhelmed looking at all of it that my brain just started haphazardly picking up things to get done even if it wasn't as important. All my regular self soothing mechanisms were not working. I called a friend, diagnosed the problem, and made several views for myself so that I am seeing smaller sets things in my everyday. But it was a good lesson that I am really just a bundle of systems that work and coping mechanisms.
