They’ve finally decided to do something about the large backlog of tasks that show up when one runs a household that I’d asked them for help with over two years ago, and I’m very grateful.

He gave me the gory details of his teeth replacement surgery, and broke up a fight mum and I were having. I like having my parents in my life. I had resigned myself to a life without them knowing _me_, so this feels like such a gift.

Then I set very strict limits for myself + there was a small reorg as we moved to the next prototype which significantly changed how I think about the actual work I’m doing ([reminders I wrote for engineers using LLMs](/blog/reminders-for-engineers-using-llms)) - specifically, I’m able to think about the larger product + the larger architecture more now that I’m not sweating the details. The venue of the wedding was beautiful, pared down and gorgeous - and most importantly, there was a big board game table. I finally played a big board game after two years! [Terraforming Mars](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/167791/terraforming-mars), with [Roshni](https://roshni-ranganathan.squarespace.com/) and [Nihar](https://nihar.works/). We were the last people at the venue by about an hour, but we finished it!

- This time, when the body gave up, it took the brain with it. I lost all my mornings for at least 6 weeks. I stopped football, I stopped running, I stopped even being able to do a stretch. Things finally got better two weeks ago. But I went from being able to play 2 football games back to back (3 hour of on-off explosive running!) to not being able to spend even half an hour on the court without my core burning. Why does the body have to undergo such a drastic reset every time there is a break? I am sad but I know I have to make my peace with it. I will spend my whole life trying to walk up this hill.
- I went to this talk about pitching your voice being given by a voice therapist- specifically in the context of gender presentation. I am interested in being able to change the long term register of your voice without causing stress to your vocal cords. I had a [vocal chord injury](https://t.me/padawanjacksings/80) ten years ago now that robbed me of several octaves. It’s been a decade but it is still hard to acknowledge this; which means I haven’t been able to put effort into [“fixing it”|”working around it”].
- Anyway, the session itself made me feel like I was back in a classroom (in the worst way) but it did open my mind to lots of options that I wouldn’t have otherwise known about.
- Need to keep reminding myself that the word “harness” now has a special meaning in the LLM world. So what do I call the way I build applications now?
- I accompanied a friend to a medical procedure where they were under anesthesia. It was hilarious. I enjoy listening to my friends talk about sofas that dance in front of the door that leads to unicorn land. I also like knowing that we are there for each other like this. Me and my village against the world.
- Jalebi was under anesthesia for dental scaling (he is showing early signs of gingivitis). This was not as fun. This was happening in the middle of my worst flare and just as I was recovering from the burnout, so I scheduled 4-5 friends to come over and care give (with) me for the 48 hours after. Friends, when I tell you how unnecessary this was. The [last time](/weeknotes/week-of-oct-4th-2025) when Litchee was recovering I remember it being hell. It took over 24 hours just for the anesthetic to leave his body, and another 32 for him to be able to eat without smooshing his face against the bowl + not hide away the whole time. But Jalebi? The desire to **big yell** that courses through his veins burned any anesthetic up so fast he was up and ready to go before we even took him home. Whew. I…

- In the process of dealing with all the cat care, I referred to this blog several times, to see if I had actually done the things I’d set up on the calendar or marked as complete on the todo list. My weeknotes are a source of truth in a way that my other surfaces are not, because they help me reconstruct the _emotional state of mind_ I was in at a given point in time, in a way that mere facts do not. This means I now have a good reason to build a search feature into the site. It also means that these notes are independently useful outside of the reasons I listed here. I’m glad I took this break from writing it, because it illustrated very clearly why this was ill-advised.
